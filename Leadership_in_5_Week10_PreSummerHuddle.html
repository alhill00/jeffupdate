The Pre-Summer
Ops Huddle
Whether your summer gets busier or your team thins out — prep now.
Promote Ownership Collaboration & Teamwork
Summer at Breeze means two things: the ops tempo goes up, and vacation requests pile up. Whether you're running harder or running leaner — your team's capacity is about to be tested. Take 5 minutes and close the gaps before they find you first.
Your 5-Point Pre-Summer Checklist
1
Coverage gaps are identified and owned.
Do you know who's off, when, and who covers? Surprises on your busiest days start here.
2
Your team knows the top 2–3 priorities for summer.
When pace picks up, people default to what they think matters. Make sure they're right.
3
Your communication rhythm is set.
Brief daily or weekly syncs prevent 10 separate "quick questions." Schedule them now.
4
Decision authority is clear when you're unreachable.
Who calls the audible if you're in the air, on a call, or unavailable? Your team shouldn't have to guess.
5
You have a plan to protect your team's energy.
Summer burns people out. What's one thing you'll do to keep your team running strong?
