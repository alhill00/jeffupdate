The GROW Coaching Model

The most powerful coaching session you'll ever run takes 10 minutes.

If you know the four questions.

GROW is one of the most widely used coaching frameworks in the world — and one of the most underused in day-to-day leadership. It's not therapy and it's not a performance review. It's a structured conversation that helps your Team Member think more clearly about where they're going and what's actually in the way.

The Tool

The GROW Model

G

Goal

"What do you want to achieve?" Get specific. Not "be better at communication" — "be able to present to leadership without feeling rattled." Clarity on the goal makes everything else easier.

R

Reality

"Where are you right now?" What's actually happening, not the story about what's happening. This is about honest assessment — what's working, what's not, and what resources are available.

O

Options

"What could you do?" Generate at least 3 options before evaluating any. Quantity first, quality second. Wild ideas are welcome — they often unlock realistic ones.

W

Will (Way Forward)

"What WILL you do, and by when?" Commitment, not intention. The difference between "I'll try" and "I will" is where accountability lives.

 

Sound Familiar?

A Team Member tells you they want to be ready for a supervisor role in 18 months. You say "that's great, keep doing what you're doing" and move on to the next agenda item.

That's support without coaching. A GROW conversation in that same moment could have produced a development plan in 10 minutes. The goal was mentioned. The work never started.

Your Challenge This Week

Run one GROW conversation this week — doesn't have to be long or formal. Find someone with a goal and walk through the four questions. You'll be surprised how much clarity 10 minutes of good questions creates. The coaching session you keep meaning to have? Have it this week.
 

Quick Pulse — 1 Question
