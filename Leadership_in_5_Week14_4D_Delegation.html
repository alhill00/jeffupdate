The 4D
Framework

A smarter way to manage your time and grow your team in under 5 minutes.

01 of 04
D
Do
Tasks that require your expertise and your authority. These are the things only you can do — or that you do significantly better than anyone else.
"This performance conversation needs to come from me directly."
02 of 04
D
Delegate
Tasks someone else can do — and that will stretch them. Delegation isn't offloading. It's developing. Give them the what and the why, not just the task.
"Can you own the schedule build this month? Here's why it matters and what good looks like."
03 of 04
D
Defer
Important but not urgent. These tasks need a home on your calendar — not your "I'll get to it" list. Block time or they'll never happen.
"Updating the SOP matters, but it's not this week. Blocking Thursday the 16th."
04 of 04
D
Drop
The hardest D. These are tasks that feel productive but don't move the needle. If it's not aligned with your goals or your team's priorities — let it go.
"That recurring meeting with no agenda? Cancel it. Free up 30 minutes for real work."

Are you Dumping or Delegating?

Quick self-audit — be honest with yourself.

Dumping ✕ Delegating ✓
✕ "Just handle this" ✓ "Here's the goal and why it matters"
✕ No context given ✓ Shares background and expectations
✕ Disappears after handoff ✓ Checks in without micromanaging
✕ Only passes the unpleasant tasks ✓ Passes growth opportunities
✕ Takes credit for the result ✓ Gives credit and celebrates
