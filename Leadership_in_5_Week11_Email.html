The 30-Second
Check-In

One phrase that replaces "how are you" — and actually gets a real answer.
Communicate Openly | Communicating Powerfully

Most check-ins are a formality. "How are you?" gets "Fine." "How's the team?" gets "Good." You asked, they answered, nothing changed. This week, swap that one phrase for something that gets a real answer — in 30 seconds or less.

The Swap
Stop Saying
"How are you?"
→
Start Saying
"What's one thing I can clear for you?"
Why It Works
1

It's specific — "one thing"

Asking for one thing removes the pressure of spilling everything. It gives permission to share one real issue, not a performance. You get a focused, honest answer instead of a shrug.

2

It's action-oriented — "I can clear"

You're not asking how they feel. You're offering to remove an obstacle. That reframes you as a resource, not just a manager checking a box. It signals: I'm here to help, not just to ask.

3

It surfaces real blockers

The obstacles slowing your team down usually live in the gap between "I'm fine" and what they'd actually say if asked directly. This question opens that gap — comfortably.

The 30-Second Structure

0 – 10 sec

Stop and face them. Put down whatever you're carrying. Eye contact. The pause itself signals: you have my attention.

10 – 15 sec

Ask the question. "What's one thing I can clear for you right now?" Say it like you mean it.

15 – 30 sec

Actually listen — don't move yet. Resist filling the silence. The answer is coming. Let it arrive.

This Week's Challenge

Use "What's one thing I can clear for you?"
in at least 3 conversations this week.
Notice what you hear that you wouldn't have heard otherwise.

Weekly Reflection

"Try replacing 'how are you' with 'what's one thing I can clear for you' this week. What did you hear?"
