What pressure reveals
about you as a leader

Do What's Right  ·  Character  ·  June 24, 2026
Do What's Right  |  Character

It's Friday at 4:45 PM.

Your ops are behind, your team is exhausted, and someone just made a mistake that's about to ripple — into another department, a business partner, or your own team. Your manager is asking for an update in 10 minutes.

What do you do first?
A
Get the facts and protect your team — figure out what happened before pointing fingers.
B
Escalate fast and make sure leadership knows it wasn't your call.
· · · · · · · · · · · · · · · · · ·
The Answer
If you said A — that's character under pressure.

Here's the truth: pressure doesn't create who you are as a leader. It reveals it.

The version of you that shows up at 4:45 on a bad Friday? That's the leader your team actually knows. Not the polished version from your best day.

Three questions to sit with this week
  1. When things get hard, do you protect your team or protect yourself?
  2. Do you slow down to get it right, or speed up to look right?
  3. What would your team say you did — without you in the room?
Your Challenge This Week The next time pressure spikes, pause for 10 seconds before responding. Notice what your instinct was. That's your data.
Reflection Question Think of a high-pressure moment this week. What did it reveal about you as a leader?
